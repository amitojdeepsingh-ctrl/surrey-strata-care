Questions

What managers and landlords ask first

How is pricing set?

By scope, not by the hour. We walk the building, write a scope sheet with you, and return a fixed monthly price for recurring work or a fixed per-visit price for turnovers and post-construction. The quote is valid for 90 days.

Are you insured and registered?

Yes. WorkSafeBC registration and commercial general liability coverage are in place before the first crew member works on a site. A certificate of insurance is available on request for your file.

How do you handle keys and alarm codes?

Every key and fob is entered in a numbered register, issued against a signed receipt, and returned the same way when the contract ends. Alarm codes are held by the owner and the assigned lead only, and lockup is recorded on the visit sheet.

What happens if something is missed?

Report it and it is corrected within 48 hours at no charge. The monthly scorecard tracks corrections so the same item does not keep recurring without a change to the scope or the route.

Do you take on one-off jobs?

Yes — turnovers, move-outs, and post-construction cleans are quoted per job. Recurring strata and small-office routes are quoted as annual agreements with monthly billing.
