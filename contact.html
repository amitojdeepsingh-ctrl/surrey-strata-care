Before you call

Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

Same day for calls and texts sent during business hours, and by the next morning for anything sent overnight. A walk-through is usually booked within two business days.

Do you need a site walk before quoting?

For strata and post-construction work, yes — the scope sheet is written on site. For a standard turnover we can quote from photos and the unit size, with the price confirmed on arrival if the condition differs.

What do I get in writing?

A scope sheet listing every task and exclusion, a fixed price, the visit frequency, and the correction window. After each visit: a time-stamped photo report. Monthly: a quality scorecard.
