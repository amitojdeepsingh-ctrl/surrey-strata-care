A cleaning company built on paperwork

Surrey Strata & Commercial Care is a Mission-based, owner-operated cleaning company. The owner runs the routes, writes the quotes, and signs the key register.

The owner

Swaranjit Singh

I came to cleaning work through building services, not through a franchise purchase. I have worked on residential and commercial sites in the Lower Mainland since 2024 — site cleanup around live trades, deficiency detail work, and security shifts with key custody and lockup responsibility.

Two things from that work shaped this company. First, a building is judged by the evidence someone can produce: photos, meter reads, a signed key receipt, a dated report. Second, most small cleaning operations lose contracts because the paperwork is thin, not because the cleaning is poor.

Before Canada, I worked in a law chamber handling client intake, drafting, and file discipline. That is where the habit of writing things down comes from — and it is why every job here starts with a written scope sheet and ends with a report.

I speak Punjabi, Hindi, and English, which matters in a corridor where a lot of strata councils and landlords prefer to explain a problem in the language they think in.

Operating standards

How the work is held to a standard

A

Scope before price

Every quote is built from a scope sheet written on site. The task list, frequency, and exclusions are agreed in writing before work starts, and the price does not move unless the scope does.

B

Evidence after work

Time-stamped photos go out after each visit. Recurring clients get a monthly scorecard showing the visits, the corrections, and any scope changes — the document a council needs at renewal.

C

Keys with receipts

Keys and fobs are numbered, issued against signed receipts, and logged on return. Alarm codes stay with the owner and the assigned lead. Lockup is recorded on the visit sheet, window checks included.

Community

Hiring and giving back in the corridor

Entry-level cleaning jobs here include paid WHMIS and safety training, written references, and a route to a lead-hand role. Openings are posted on the federal Job Bank so local and newcomer applicants see them.

We also hold two pro-bono common-area refresh days each quarter for seniors' stratas and small non-profits — capped so they never displace paid service work.

Practical commitments

  • Paid safety training for every hire, logged and signed
  • Four-week onboarding before a new cleaner works alone
  • Eco-certified chemistry with safety data sheets on site
  • Recycling sorted to local municipal rules
  • Two pro-bono refresh days per quarter, by application
