Where we clean

A corridor, not a franchise map. We stay inside one working radius so arrival times and quality do not depend on traffic luck.

Route discipline

Why the radius is deliberately small

A cleaner who drives 90 minutes to the first job is already behind on the second. We cap the corridor at roughly 90 minutes end to end, schedule east-end work on two fixed days, and keep Mission and Dewdney same-day.

  • Same dayMission, Hatzic, Dewdney — turnovers and urgent items.
  • Next dayAgassiz, Harrison Mills, Hope — on the Tuesday and Thursday route.
  • By agreementEastern Surrey and North Langley for recurring routes where the route fits the schedule.

Outside the corridor?

For post-construction and one-time detail work we will quote a larger project outside the corridor when the schedule allows it. Recurring agreements outside the radius are declined at the quote stage rather than accepted and under-served later.
